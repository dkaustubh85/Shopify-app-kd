App name: StockSheet
Introduction: Update inventory from spreadsheets with previews and quantity undo.

Description:
Upload an Excel or CSV spreadsheet, map its columns, and choose an inventory location. Preview matched and unmatched rows before confirming updates. Track progress in History and download your original file or results. For inventory quantities, undo restores saved values while skipping stock that changed after the update. Price updates cannot be undone automatically.

Feature copy:
- Import Excel or CSV files and map spreadsheet columns.
- Preview inventory updates before confirming changes.
- Choose the inventory location for your update.
- Download original uploads and results from History.
- Undo inventory quantities with checks for later changes.
