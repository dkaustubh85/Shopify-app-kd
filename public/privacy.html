StockSheet privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

StockSheet is operated by kaustubh. This notice explains how information is handled when merchants install and use StockSheet to manage inventory and product prices. Contact dkaustubh85@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we process

  • Shop domain, app settings, Shopify authorization credentials and session information, including staff identifiers and account information supplied by Shopify during authentication.
  • Product and variant identifiers, titles, SKUs, prices, inventory quantities and locations used for matching, previews, exports and updates.
  • Original uploaded spreadsheets, parsed rows, saved mappings, update results and quantity snapshots used for history and undo.
  • Support correspondence and privacy-request information supplied by you or Shopify, and technical request and error information needed to operate the service.

StockSheet does not request Shopify Customers or Orders API access. Spreadsheets may nevertheless contain personal information if you put it there. Upload inventory data only; do not include customer information or sensitive personal data. Only the first worksheet is processed for updates, but the entire original file is retained so you can download it from History.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate your store, match and validate spreadsheet rows, preview and perform the updates you request, export inventory, preserve update history, protect newer stock changes during quantity undo, provide support, secure the service and fulfil privacy requests. We do not sell your information or use uploaded spreadsheets for advertising.

Service providers and access

Shopify supplies app authentication and commerce APIs. Render hosts the app and its PostgreSQL database. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. kaustubh may access relevant information to provide support, maintain the service and fulfil privacy requests. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. Provider infrastructure may process information outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Shop settings, uploaded originals, parsed rows, cached product information and update history are retained while the app is installed to support its features. You may request earlier deletion by email. After Shopify sends an authenticated shop-redaction event, StockSheet deletes that shop's active app records, sessions and uploaded files. Uninstalling is not an immediate backup purge.

Render database recovery copies age out within its recovery window, currently up to seven days for paid databases. Operational log retention follows the Render workspace plan, currently seven, fourteen or thirty days under standard plans. Recovery copies are restricted to disaster recovery. Before restoring data to service, applicable deletion requests must be reapplied. A hashed webhook-event identifier and timestamp are retained to prevent duplicate processing; this record does not contain the webhook payload.

Privacy-request details and support correspondence are retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Unnecessary personal request payloads are removed after completion. Minimal completion records may be kept to document fulfilment.

Your requests and choices

Contact dkaustubh85@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, or ask about processing. Include your shop domain and enough information to identify the request; do not email passwords or access tokens. We may verify your identity and authority before acting. Customers of a merchant should normally contact that merchant, who can submit a Shopify privacy request.

kaustubh is responsible for reviewing the privacy-request queue daily. Shopify customer requests require review of merchant-supplied files and are tracked with a thirty-day due date. Receiving a webhook does not by itself complete a request. Applicable rights and deadlines depend on your location.

Security and changes

StockSheet uses HTTPS, Shopify authentication and restricted infrastructure access. No service can guarantee absolute security. We will update this notice when our practices change and show the new effective date here.
