Your spreadsheet. Your inventory. In sync.

Meet StockSheet.

Start with an Excel or CSV file. Upload your sheet, then match its columns to your store.

Choose what to update, and select your inventory location.

Preview the changes before anything is applied. Review unmatched rows and correct your sheet when needed.

When everything looks right, confirm your changes. Follow the progress as StockSheet updates your inventory.

Need your file again? Open History to download your original spreadsheet or the results.

For inventory quantities, undo can restore saved values while skipping stock that changed later.

StockSheet. Less repetitive work. More control over your stock.
